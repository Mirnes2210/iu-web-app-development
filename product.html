Description

The AuroraBrew Smart Kettle is a 1.7-litre electric kettle with app control and temperature settings from 40 °C to 100 °C. It keeps the water warm for up to two hours.

  • Precise temperature control (40-100 °C)
  • Keep-warm function (2 hours)
  • Wi-Fi and smartphone app control
  • Automatic shut-off and boil-dry protection
